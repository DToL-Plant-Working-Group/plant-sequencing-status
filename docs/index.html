GenomeScope derived length from PacBio raw data plotted against the ungapped assembly length taken from the assembled genome. There appear to be two lines, one falling roughly on the 1:1 line, and one on the 2:1 line.

  • Colour by assembly level

The second plot shows the same data as the first plot but you can adjust the scales and colour either by heterozygosity or repeat content, both of which are derived from the GenomeScope data.

The last plot shows the 1C content on the y axis, and the ungapped fasta length from the genome assembly on the x axis. The scale can be adjusted to show more or less data. Curiously, the data lies mainly above the 1:1 line.

Data: all_genome_size_data.tsv, regenerated weekly by the plant-sequencing-status pipeline.
